Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How long does Member Registration approval take?

Registration requests are typically reviewed by library administrators within 24–48 working hours. Once approved, you will receive an activation email with your assigned Library Card ID.

2. What is the standard loan borrowing duration?

Standard loans are issued for 14 calendar days for undergraduates and 28 days for postgraduate researchers. Overnight reference books must be returned within 3 days.

3. How does the Waitlist / Book Reservation queue work?

When all physical copies of a book are currently lent out (Copies: 0), you can click "Join Waitlist". The moment another student returns a copy at the circulation desk, the system reserves it for you and holds it at the desk for 48 hours.

4. How do I access Soft Copies and E-Books?

On each book's detail page, if a digital edition is available, an authorized read-only Google Drive / PDF preview link will be provided for online viewing.
